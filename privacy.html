Poly Translator

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

The short version: Poly collects nothing. There is no account, no server of ours, and nothing you translate leaves your phone except in the ways Apple's own features work, described below.

What we collect

Nothing. Poly has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting and no third-party code of any kind. We never see your translations, your voice, your photos or how you use the app, because none of it is ever sent to us. We have no server.

What stays on your phone

Everything the app remembers is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage, and is deleted when you delete the app:

  • Your translation history
  • Phrases you star
  • Trips you create
  • Your chosen and recently used languages
  • Your settings, and whether you have bought Poly Pro

You can clear any of this inside the app: Settings → History, Saved phrases and Recent languages each have a Clear button beside the list it removes.

Camera, microphone and speech

Poly asks for these only when you use the feature that needs them, and only while you are using it.

  • Camera — used to read text from signs and menus. The image is processed live on your device and is not stored or transmitted. Poly never reads your photo library; it can only add a picture to it if you choose to save one.
  • Microphone and speech recognition — used to turn what is said into text so it can be translated. Transcription runs on your device using Apple's on-device speech models. Poly does not record, keep or upload audio.

You can withdraw either permission at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Translation

Translation is performed by Apple's Translation framework on your device. The first time you use a language, iOS downloads that language pack from Apple, which needs an internet connection; after that, translation works offline. The same is true of the on-device speech models. These downloads are handled by iOS and are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Poly Pro is bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. Poly only asks Apple whether this Apple ID is entitled to Pro, and stores that yes-or-no answer on your device.

Sharing

When you share a translation, a scan or a trip recap, iOS's own share sheet opens and you choose where it goes. What happens to it afterwards is up to the app or service you picked.

Children

Poly is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to show, export or delete on request. Everything the app knows is on your phone and under your control: clear it in Settings, or delete the app to remove all of it at once.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
